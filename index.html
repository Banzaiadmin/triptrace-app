    Decision support for professional flight crews — not validated software, and not legal or contractual advice. The fatigue call is the pilot's authority.

    Sleep is modeled from the schedule unless you import measured data. Contractual minimum rest is a scheduling floor, not a fitness guarantee. Your own symptoms — microsleeps, fixation, missed radio calls, poor crosscheck — outrank every number in this app.
