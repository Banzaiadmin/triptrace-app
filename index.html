      About TripTrace
      
      
    
      
    
      
      
      
        TripTrace reads your Trip Board, rebuilds the trip on three clocks — Zulu, station local,
        and your body clock — then models the sleep the schedule actually allows and estimates
        where your effectiveness bottoms out.
      
      
      
      
        
      1 · Pick your airline
      
        
      Each carrier's format is parsed separately. UPS is live. The others are listed so you
          know they're coming, not because they half-work.
      
      
      
      
      
        
      2 · Add your schedule
      
        
      Screenshot the Trip Board. Times are read as Zulu, cross-checked against the local-hour
          parenthetical, and anything ambiguous is flagged rather than guessed.
      
      
      
      
      
        
      3 · Add your sleep
      
        
      Import from Whoop, Oura, or an Apple Health export. Sessions are matched to layovers by
          overlap in UTC, so it works across time zones without you telling it anything. Measured
          sleep always overrides the model.
      
      
      
      
      
        
      4 · Read your report
      
        
      The ring is the trip's lowest estimated effectiveness. Each duty period gets its own
          trace from report to release.
      
      
      
      
      
        
      5 · Proposed safety report
      
        
      A formal write-up — schedule facts, physiology with citations, and a fitness-for-duty
          statement. Objective by design, so it reads as documentation rather than complaint.
      
      
      
      
      What the colors mean
      
      
      
      
      
        0.04 BAC is the FAR 91.17 limit for flying with alcohol on board. When a fatigue estimate
        reaches the same impairment level, it deserves the same respect.
      
      
      
      
        
      Good to know
      
        
      Estimates, not validated software. Sleep is modeled
          unless you import measured data. Contractual minimum rest is a
          scheduling floor, not a fitness guarantee — and a fatigue call is a normal professional
          tool. Your own symptoms always outrank any number on this screen.
