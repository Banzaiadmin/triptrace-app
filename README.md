# TripTrace — the app

This repository holds the installable web app only: what a browser downloads. It is published
automatically from the private source repository; do not edit here.

Open **https://banzaiadmin.github.io/triptrace-app/** on your phone. iPhone: Safari → Share → *Add to Home Screen*. Android: Chrome →
*Install*. Everything runs on the device: paste a Trip Board, open a sample, log delays and
conditions as the trip unfolds, export the fatigue summary. Screenshots are read on the device too.

TripTrace is SAFTE-style decision support — not validated biomathematical software, and not legal
or contractual advice. The fatigue call is the pilot's authority.
